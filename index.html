How it works

Staking Ethereum, without the complexity

EtherStake handles the validator infrastructure so you earn rewards with full transparency — no minimum beyond 0.01 ETH, no lockup surprises.

1

Connect your wallet

Link any EVM-compatible wallet — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, or WalletConnect. No account creation required.

2

Deposit ETH

Choose how much to stake. We aggregate deposits across validators, so you're not waiting for a full 32 ETH threshold.

3

Earn rewards

Rewards accrue every epoch. Withdraw anytime — full or partial — with no exit queue surprises on our end.

Built on Ethereum's proof-of-stake protocol

EtherStake runs non-custodial validator nodes distributed across multiple cloud regions and bare-metal providers. Your ETH never leaves your control — we operate the infrastructure, you hold the keys.

Smart contracts are audited by third-party security firms and open-sourced on GitHub. Every validator's performance is tracked and published in real time.

Daily Rewards

Two tiers, one goal — maximum returns

EtherStake pays rewards daily, directly to your staking balance. Your rate depends on how long your ETH has been staked — higher in the early months, sustained through the long term.

Tier 1 — Early Staker
0.9%
per day on your staked ETH
Months 1 – 6 (Days 1–180)
  • Rewards credited to your balance every 24 hours, compounding automatically.
  • On 1 ETH staked, you earn 0.009 ETH per day — roughly 1.63 ETH over 6 months.
  • No action needed — rewards accumulate while your ETH stays in the vault.
  • Early stakers get the highest rate as a reward for providing early liquidity to the protocol.
  • Rate applies to your original principal plus any compounded rewards already credited.
Tier 5 — Long-Term Staker
0.45%
per day on your staked ETH
Months 7 – 12 (Days 181–365)
  • Rate automatically transitions to 0.45%/day on Day 181 — no action required.
  • On 1 ETH staked, you earn 0.0045 ETH per day — roughly 0.83 ETH over the second 6 months.
  • By end of month 12, a 1 ETH stake will have generated approximately 2.46 ETH in total rewards.
  • Long-term stakers support network stability — this tier rewards your continued commitment.
  • Rewards continue compounding on your full balance including all Tier 1 earnings.

Automatic tier transition — no action needed

Your staking tier is tracked from the moment your deposit is confirmed on-chain. On Day 181, your rate transitions from 0.9% to 0.45% daily automatically. You will receive an email notification 7 days before the transition. After Day 365, rewards continue at 0.45%/day indefinitely until you choose to withdraw.
